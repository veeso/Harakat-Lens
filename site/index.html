About Harakat Lens

Harakat Lens is a native iOS app for reading, transliterating, and translating Arabic text on the go. Point your camera at a sign, book, menu, or any printed Arabic script and the built-in OCR engine extracts the text in real time, then renders it in Latin characters with full harakat (diacritic) support so you can pronounce every word correctly — even if you do not yet read the Arabic alphabet.

Designed for travelers, students of Arabic, and Muslims around the world, Harakat Lens also includes a dedicated Quran mode that automatically detects and matches scanned verses against the full Quranic text, displaying the canonical reading alongside the translation. On-device translation keeps your scans private and works without an internet connection once language packs are downloaded.

Whether you are decoding street signs in Cairo, studying Modern Standard Arabic, or deepening your understanding of the Quran, Harakat Lens turns your phone into a fast, accurate Arabic reading companion — powered by Apple Vision.
